3. Reading: No More Boring Stopovers!

Few things are more depressing than spending hours in an airport terminal waiting for a connecting flight. You are sitting in the departure lounge watching the clock, eating overpriced snacks, and wishing you were anywhere else.

Frenchman Emmanuel Rozenblum and his sister Anna were on a stopover at Warsaw's main airport. They realized that outside the airport was a vibrant European city with fantastic museums, restaurants, and sights. However, they were terrified of getting lost or missing their flight, so they stayed in the terminal.

That frustrating experience inspired them to launch a startup called TripAside. The service arranges personalized guided tours during long connections. A local guide picks travellers up right outside the terminal, takes them on a mini-tour of the city center, and guarantees to bring them back on time. Started in Paris, TripAside has now expanded to London, Rome, Frankfurt, and Madrid.

4. Grammar: be going to (Plans & Predictions)

Grammar Rule: be going to + Infinitive

1. Future Plans and Intentions: Use when a decision was made before the moment of speaking.

• We are going to book a tour with TripAside tomorrow.
• He isn't going to spend his stopover in the lounge.


2. Predictions based on present evidence: Use when you can see right now that something will happen.

• Look at those dark clouds! It is going to rain.
• The gate is closing! You are going to miss your plane!

Exercise 4.1: Plan or Prediction?
Decide whether each sentence expresses a Plan (PL) or a Prediction based on evidence (PR):
  1. Look at the departure board: flight BA204 says "DELAYED". They are going to arrive late. → [ ]
  2. I've saved enough money. I'm going to travel to Japan this autumn. → [ ]
  3. She is holding 4 big bags and can barely walk. She's going to drop them! → [ ]
  4. We aren't going to eat at the airport café because it's too expensive. → [ ]
Show answers
1. PR (evidence on board) | 2. PL (plan/decision) | 3. PR (evidence) | 4. PL (plan/intention)

5. Speaking
Task 5.1: Plan Your Stopover Tour (Role-Play)
Imagine you have an 8-hour stopover in one of these cities: Paris, London, Istanbul, or Rome. Discuss your plan with your tutor using be going to:
  • Which city did you choose and what time are you leaving the airport?
  • What 2–3 places are you going to see?
  • Where and what are you going to eat?
  • What time are you going to return to the airport to avoid missing your flight?
Task 5.2: TripAside Pitch
Imagine you work for TripAside and you must convince a tired, nervous passenger to leave the airport terminal and take your tour:
  • Address their fear: "What if we get stuck in traffic and miss the flight?"
  • Describe what exciting things they are going to experience in the city instead of sitting in the terminal.
